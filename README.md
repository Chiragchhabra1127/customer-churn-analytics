# Customer Churn Analytics
---

## 🎯 Project Overview

AI-powered analysis of **94,500+ customers** and **3,500+ exit interviews** to identify churn drivers and provide actionable retention strategies for a SaaS company.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Total Customers** | 94,526 |
| **Churned Customers** | 19,656 (20.79%) |
| **Monthly Revenue at Risk** | $706,868 |
| **Exit Interviews Analyzed** | 3,285 |
| **Data Quality** | 100% |

---

## 🔴 Top Churn Drivers

1. **Support Issues** (40.1%) - Slow response times
2. **Product Issues** (19.9%) - Reliability problems
3. **Price Sensitivity** (19.7%) - Cost concerns
4. **Competitor Pressure** (8.0%) - Better alternatives
5. **Others** (12.3%) - Performance, value, onboarding

---

## 💡 5 Strategic Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Impact | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Support Crisis** - Hire 25-30% more staff | +400-500 customers | Immediate |
| 2 | **Early Engagement** - Enhanced onboarding | +1,100 customers | 1-2 months |
| 3 | **Price Strategy** - Flexible plans | +300-400 customers | 2-3 months |
| 4 | **Product Reliability** - Performance improvements | +150-200 customers | 3-6 months |
| 5 | **Regional Strategy** - Localization | Geographic optimization | Ongoing |

**Expected Annual Impact:** $2.1M - $4.2M revenue recovery
